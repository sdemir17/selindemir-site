English Language Teaching · Generative AI · Teacher Education

Selin Demir

PhD candidate · Lecturer · Teacher trainer

I am a PhD candidate in English Language Education at Bahçeşehir University, where my dissertation examines how English teachers develop critical generative AI literacy. I hold a BA and an MA in English Language Teaching from Maltepe University, graduating with honours, and I studied English Language and Culture at Tallinn University.

I work as a lecturer and hold the CELTA. As a teacher trainer, I design and run in-service programs for English teachers.
